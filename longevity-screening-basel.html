Basel is not a casual place to talk about longevity science. Aeonix's Longevity Plus panel brings that same evidence-based rigor to a physician-reviewed blood panel available directly to Basel residents.

135+ biomarkers, results in 48 hours, no single invented "age score" — just the individual biological systems the longevity research field actually agrees matter.

Why longevity screening matters in Basel

Basel's pharma and biotech workforce is unusually well-informed about the biology of ageing — many read the primary research their own employers publish. That awareness creates a specific expectation: a screening product that reflects the current evidence, not a wellness-industry shortcut. A standard GP check-up in Switzerland tests 12–20 markers once every three years, which is not enough to track the slow-moving biological signals — vascular ageing, insulin resistance, chronic inflammation, hormonal decline — that most influence how many healthy years you actually get.

What Aeonix's Longevity Plus panel measures

The panel is built around the biomarker categories with the strongest published evidence base for healthy-ageing risk — deliberately the same underlying biology that academic "biological age" research draws on, without collapsing it into one number:

  • Cardiovascular ageing: ApoB, Lp(a), full lipid panel, hsCRP, IL-6
  • Metabolic function: fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, HbA1c, fasting glucose
  • Hormonal balance: full thyroid axis (TSH, free T3, free T4, Anti-TPO), DHEA-S, cortisol, sex hormones
  • Nutritional status: Vitamin D, Omega-3 Index, B12, folate, iron, ferritin, zinc, magnesium
  • Cancer adjunct and organ markers: PSA (men), CA-125 (women), CEA, plus renal and hepatic function
