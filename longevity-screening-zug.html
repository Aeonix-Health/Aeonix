Why longevity screening matters in Zug

Founders and executives in Crypto Valley tend to have irregular schedules, high sustained stress, and little patience for a GP waiting room. A standard Swiss check-up tests 12–20 markers once every three years — nowhere near enough to catch the slow-moving biological drift (vascular ageing, insulin resistance, chronic inflammation, hormonal decline) that determines how many productive years you actually get. Longevity screening exists to catch that drift early, while it's still reversible.

What Aeonix's Longevity Plus panel measures

Longevity Plus is built around the biomarker categories with the strongest evidence base for healthy-ageing risk — real, physician-reviewed values rather than a single proprietary "age score":

  • Cardiovascular ageing: ApoB, Lp(a), full lipid panel, hsCRP, IL-6
  • Metabolic function: fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, HbA1c, fasting glucose
  • Hormonal balance: full thyroid axis (TSH, free T3, free T4, Anti-TPO), DHEA-S, cortisol, sex hormones
  • Nutritional status: Vitamin D, Omega-3 Index, B12, folate, iron, ferritin, zinc, magnesium
  • Cancer adjunct and organ markers: PSA (men), CA-125 (women), CEA, plus renal and hepatic function
