Why longevity screening matters in Zürich

A standard Swiss GP check-up (Vorsorgeuntersuchung) tests 12–20 markers once every three years — enough to catch overt disease, not enough to track the slow biological drift that precedes it. The conditions most likely to shorten a healthy career — cardiovascular disease, insulin resistance, chronic inflammation, hormonal decline — develop silently over years and are almost always correctable if caught early. For Zürich's demanding professional population, longevity screening is less about anxiety and more about extending the years you're actually able to perform at your best.

What Aeonix's Longevity Plus panel measures

Longevity Plus is built around the biomarker categories with the strongest evidence base for healthy-ageing risk — the same systems that most "biological age" formulas are themselves derived from, without reducing your results to a single invented score:

  • Cardiovascular ageing: ApoB, Lp(a), full lipid panel, hsCRP, IL-6 — vascular health is one of the strongest predictors of healthspan
  • Metabolic function: fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, HbA1c, fasting glucose — insulin resistance accelerates cellular ageing years before it appears on a standard panel
  • Hormonal balance: full thyroid axis (TSH, free T3, free T4, Anti-TPO), DHEA-S, cortisol, sex hormones
  • Nutritional status: Vitamin D, B12, folate, iron, ferritin, zinc, magnesium
  • Cancer adjunct and bone/organ markers: PSA (men), CEA, AFP, plus renal and hepatic function panels
